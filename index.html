          Cubes Table

          a³ ± b³
          0 chips
          Pick a level

          Factor a sum or a difference of cubes. A wrong answer never costs you chips.

          Remember

          Sum: a³ + b³ = (a + b)(a² − ab + b²)

          Difference: a³ − b³ = (a − b)(a² + ab + b²)

          The signs: Same, Opposite, Always positive.
